Reading this page

OK Publishing within tolerance of the nominal rate.
SLOW Publishing, but below tolerance. The node is running and falling behind — usually CPU contention or a blocked callback.
FAST Above tolerance. Informational; normally means the nominal figure here is stale relative to a retuned timer.
DEAD No publisher on the graph, or nothing received recently enough to measure. Check the node actually started.

Topics with no nominal rate are event-driven — /cmd_vel only publishes while something is driving — so they are judged on staleness alone and never read SLOW.
