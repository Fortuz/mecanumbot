Teleop

Drive the robot, move the neck and grabbers, and set the LEDs from the browser. Driving is dead-man: the robot only moves while Hold to drive is held, and a command lasts {{ '%.1f'|format(limits.lease_seconds) }} s unless the page renews it, so letting go, switching tab or losing Wi-Fi stops the wheels.

Set the three speeds, then press and hold Hold to drive (or hold Space). The scales can be moved while driving. Limits are the joystick's: ±{{ '%.3f'|format(limits.max_lin_vel) }} m/s, ±{{ '%.3f'|format(limits.max_ang_vel) }} rad/s. Commands go to /cmd_vel directly, bypassing Nav2 and its costmap: nothing here avoids obstacles.

Neck and grabbers

The sliders start at the joystick profile's defaults, not at where the servos are: nothing is sent until one is moved, and then all three positions go out together on /cmd_accessory_pos. A connected joystick re-sends its own positions once a second and will take the servos back.

Nothing is sent until Apply. Changed corners are outlined. set_led_status always carries all four corners, so a corner you did not change is sent as it is now, so it stays as it is. Under each corner is what the controller last reported. A running behaviour tree drives the LEDs too and will overwrite this.
